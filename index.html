01 • ARCHITECTURAL COMMISSION

THE BRIEF

Conceived for a multi-generational family in Greater Noida, the client sought a serene sanctuary that shields living spaces from intense North Indian summer radiation while inviting ambient natural daylight deep into the interior floorplan.

The structural solution eliminates typical partition walls in favor of double-height spatial volumes anchored by a central monolithic travertine spine. Private suites hover above the living sanctuary, connected via cantilevered steel and smoked oak galleries.

FIG. 02 — STRUCTURAL AXONOMETRIC & SOLAR AXIS DIAGRAM
02 • SPATIAL RIGOR

DESIGN APPROACH

Rather than applying decorative surface finishes, we engineered passive thermal insulation directly into the building envelope using monolithic lime-washed masonry and custom fluted oak acoustic louvers.

Daylight is sculpted through rhythmic vertical fins that cast slowly shifting linear shadows across the natural stone floor throughout the day. Acoustic dampening is seamlessly integrated into wall cavities behind micro-perforated smoked oak timber panels.
